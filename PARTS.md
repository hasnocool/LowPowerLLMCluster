## Reading the catalog

The machine-readable catalog keeps source type, software maturity, risk, lifecycle, power scope and host requirements. Use `data/parts.json` as the manifest and the files in `data/catalog/` as the editable source records.

A specialist accelerator is not a failed LLM node: Coral/MemryX-class hardware can still save whole-cluster energy by keeping larger workers asleep. Conversely, an EOL FPGA/ASIC with impressive TOPS stays a research/watch item until a real runtime, price and complete-node benchmark exist.
