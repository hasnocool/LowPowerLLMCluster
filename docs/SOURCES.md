## Interpretation rules

A marketplace page is evidence of a current advertised listing, not a guarantee of configuration, availability, authenticity or final landed price. When a page contains multiple variants, `price_min_usd` and `price_max_usd` describe the advertised range visible at verification time unless a specific configuration price is clearly resolved.
